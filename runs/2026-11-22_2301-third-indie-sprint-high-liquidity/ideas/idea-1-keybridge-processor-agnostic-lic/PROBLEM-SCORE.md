# Problem Score — Keybridge — Processor-Agnostic License Key, Entitlement & Signed-Release Control Plane

ID: `IDEA-1` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-14

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Software founders and 1-10 engineer dev shops selling paid macOS/Windows/Linux desktop apps, WordPress/Figma/Obsidian/VS Code plugins, or commercial CLI tools via license keys—particularly vendors migrating off Lemon Squeezy before its March 31, 2027 shutdown or routing checkouts across Stripe, Paddle, Polar, and PayPal.
- **Problem:** Lemon Squeezy's non-extendable March 31, 2027 shutdown strands installed license keys because Stripe Managed Payments omits native license keys, hosted release delivery, PayPal subscription migration, and sellers outside ~37 countries, while billing buyers as LINK.COM. Migrating to another bundled MoR (Polar, Dodo, Creem) repeats single-vendor lock-in, whereas standalone Keygen.sh charges $99/mo for only 1,000 active licensed users (5 products), $299/mo for 10,000 ALUs, plus $995/yr for a custom domain and provides no Lemon Squeezy drop-in compatibility or multi-MoR webhook sync.
- **Trade-offs:** No payment processing/MoR liability (webhook-only across Stripe/Paddle/Polar/Dodo/PayPal); no enterprise on-prem air-gapped FlexLM/RLM dongles or custom legal procurement (cloud + offline cryptographic files only); no affiliate marketplace, storefront builder, or email suite (strictly license validation, device caps, multi-rail entitlement sync, and signed binary/plugin update feeds).
- **Price:** $49/mo (up to 10,000 active licensed devices, unlimited products, custom validation domain included, 0% GMV fee) and $129/mo (up to 50,000 active devices, team seat management, signed release CDN) (More for Less)
