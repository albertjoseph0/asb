# Problem Score — KeystoneDock: MoR-Agnostic License, Signed Release & Branded Customer Hub for Software Vendors

ID: `IDEA-2` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-1

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Independent commercial software developers and 1-5 person product studios selling paid desktop apps (macOS/Windows/Linux via Swift, Electron, or Tauri), IDE/Figma/WordPress/Obsidian plugins, or CLI/self-hosted software on Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe, Paddle, Polar, Creem, or Dodo—with an immediate beachhead migrating off Lemon Squeezy ahead of its March 31, 2027 shutdown.
- **Problem:** Lemon Squeezy's permanent March 31, 2027 shutdown is forcing tens of thousands of software vendors onto Stripe Managed Payments, which explicitly omits hosted storefronts, software license keys, signed binary/file delivery, affiliate tracking, and PayPal (and excludes merchants outside ~37 countries). Stitching together standalone key APIs (Keymint $12-$99/mo, LicenseSeat $9-$79/mo, Keygen $49-$99/mo), separate affiliate trackers (Dub/Rewardful), and custom release/update servers leaves end-buyers without a unified portal to manage activations and downloads—while moving to another single MoR (Creem, Polar, Paddle, Freemius) repeats the exact 4-8 week vendor lock-in trap.
- **Trade-offs:** 1) Not an MoR (no payment processing, funds custody, or VAT/chargeback absorption — plugs into buyer's own Stripe Managed Payments, Stripe, Paddle, Polar, Creem, Dodo, or PayPal); 2) Strictly installable software (desktop apps, IDE/Figma/WP/Obsidian plugins, CLIs, self-hosted) skipping ebooks, courses, newsletters, and creator storefronts; 3) Flat monthly fees ($39-$89/mo) with 0% revenue share and capped free sandbox only (disqualifying sub-$1k hobbyists); 4) Developer-owned custom-domain portal & cryptographic release feeds instead of a consumer discovery marketplace.
- **Price:** $39/mo ($390/yr) Indie (up to 2,500 active licensed customers, unlimited products, 0% revenue share) and $89/mo ($890/yr) Studio (up to 15,000 active licensed customers, custom-domain portal, signed release/auto-update CDN feeds, built-in affiliates, 0% revenue share) (More for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** Seed-to-Series-B B2B developer-tool, AI/agent, security, and open-core software startups (5-50 employees) distributing self-hosted Docker Compose, Helm/Kubernetes, or air-gapped enterprise software to 10-250 paying customer organizations.
- **Problem:** B2B developer-tool, AI/agent, and open-core software startups selling self-hosted Docker Compose, Helm, or air-gapped enterprise deployments hit a brutal per-customer pricing cliff once they pass ~10 enterprise installations: Replicated starts at $2,000-$3,000/mo plus $50-$200/license/mo ($24k-$60k+/yr), Distr (Glasskube) charges $128/customer-org/mo (hitting $3,200/mo at 25 orgs and $59,000/yr Enterprise), and Nuon requires custom BYOC enterprise contracts—while cheap indie licensing APIs (LicenseSeat, Keylight) lack authenticated private OCI/Helm container registries, air-gap bundle packaging, and multi-seat enterprise customer portals.
- **Trade-offs:** 1) Deliberately skips embedded Kubernetes cluster provisioning (Replicated kURL/KOTS), remote customer-VPC Terraform orchestration (Nuon BYOC), and automated multi-cluster compatibility matrices—strictly providing an authenticated OCI/Helm/Compose release proxy, Ed25519 air-gap license files, and a custom-domain enterprise customer portal (Less for Less); 2) Flat tier pricing ($299-$699/mo for 50-250 customer orgs) with $0 per-org surcharges, disqualifying massive Fortune 500 ISVs requiring custom SAML/dedicated-infra $59k+ enterprise contracts; 3) Payment-agnostic (syncs Stripe, Paddle, or B2B invoice/PO webhooks) rather than acting as an MoR.
- **Price:** $299/mo ($3,588/yr) Growth (up to 50 self-hosted customer orgs, authenticated OCI/Helm/Docker Compose registry proxy, Ed25519 air-gap license files, custom-domain enterprise customer portal, Stripe/Paddle/invoice webhook sync, 0% per-org tax) and $699/mo ($8,388/yr) Scale (up to 250 customer orgs, automated air-gap tarball builder, org admin magic-link portal, audit logs) (Less for Less)
