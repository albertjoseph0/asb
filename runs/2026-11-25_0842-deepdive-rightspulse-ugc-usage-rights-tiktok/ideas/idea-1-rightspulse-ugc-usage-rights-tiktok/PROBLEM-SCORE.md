# Problem Score — RightsPulse (UGC Usage-Rights & TikTok Spark / Meta Partnership Expiry Guardrail)

## Scenario A
- **Buyer:** Scaling DTC e-commerce brands (in-house paid-social / growth teams) actively running 15-200+ creator-backed paid social ads (TikTok Spark Ads and/or Meta Partnership Ads and time-limited UGC) across Meta and TikTok. (Paid-social performance agencies are excluded from Scenario A as a separate buyer type per ASB single-buyer rules.)
- **Problem:** TikTok Spark Ads auth_codes (7/30/60/365-day windows) expire silently without platform email or webhook alerts, flatlining live ad sets overnight (error 4025 auth_code_expired), while Meta Partnership Ads lack native contract-expiration cutoffs—causing media buyers to keep spending on expired 30/90/180-day creator licenses and get hit with $2,000-$15,000 retroactive breach invoices surfaced via Meta Ad Library audits.
- **Trade-offs:** Standalone, strictly read-only OAuth monitor (Meta Marketing API ads_read + TikTok Marketing API identity/video/info) paired with Slack/email alerts (30d/14d/7d/48h + live-spend-on-expired-rights alarm) and zero-login creator renewal magic links. Deliberately excludes influencer discovery/scraping, creator marketplaces/take-rates, payout processing, creative ROAS analytics, and automated ad-pause write mutations (ads_management) in v1, requiring zero migration off Sheets, Motion, or Foreplay.
- **Price:** $99/mo ($990/yr) Brand plan (1 brand ad portfolio, unlimited tracked assets/codes/creators). The $249/mo ($2,490/yr) Agency plan is excluded from Scenario A to avoid blending distinct buyer types. (More for More)

## Scenario B
- **Buyer:** 8-figure scaling DTC e-commerce brands ($10M-$100M/yr revenue on Shopify Plus or multi-region storefronts) spending $50k-$500k+/mo on 50-200+ active Meta Partnership Ads and TikTok Spark Ads sourced across multiple channels (agencies, direct DMs, marketplaces, and affiliates), with an in-house VP of Growth or Creative Ops Manager. (Excludes Fortune 500 enterprises requiring SOC 2 procurement and excludes paid-social agencies.)
- **Problem:** 8-figure multi-source DTC brands run 50-200+ active creator ads sourced simultaneously from agencies, direct DMs, marketplaces, and affiliates across multiple regional Meta and TikTok ad accounts. Walled-garden platforms (Insense/Cohley) miss off-platform creators, TikTok Spark auth_codes expire silently (error 4025) killing $1,000-$5,000+/day winning ad sets mid-scale, and talent agencies actively sweep 8-figure brands via Meta Ad Library (e.g., UsageHawk) to issue $5,000-$25,000+ retroactive usage-overrun invoices on duplicated dark posts.

Deliberately excludes creator marketplaces/take-rates, influencer discovery/scraping, creator payouts, creative ROAS dashboards, and automated ad-pause write mutations (ads_management) in v1, integrating alongside existing Insense, Archive, Motion, and agency workflows without migration.
