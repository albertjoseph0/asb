# Problem Score — VampShield — Pre-Bank INR Friendly-Fraud Deflection Gate & Flat-Fee Chargeback Evidence Compiler

ID: `IDEA-2` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-10

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Operations owners and founders of physical-goods direct-to-consumer Shopify and WooCommerce/Stripe stores ($250k-$10M annual GMV, $75+ AOV) shipping tracked parcels and experiencing elevated Item-Not-Received (INR) friendly-fraud claims threatening Shopify Payments / Stripe 1.0% rolling dispute limits.
- **Problem:** Physical-goods DTC Shopify and WooCommerce/Stripe merchants face Item-Not-Received (INR) friendly-fraud claims that Shopify Protect does not cover and that push accounts toward Shopify Payments' ~1.0% rolling 90-day dispute/reserve danger zone (Note: the pitch's primary claim that $250k-$10M GMV stores face Visa VAMP's April 1, 2026 1.5% merchant cliff is factually overstated because formal VAMP merchant enrollment requires >=1,500 combined monthly fraud/dispute events, and its promise of Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0 packets for INR is legally invalid because CE 3.0 applies strictly to Visa Reason Code 10.4 Fraud, not 13.1 INR).
- **Trade-offs:** No chargeback insurance/underwriting indemnity (avoids DP10 liability transfer and 1%-1.5% GMV pricing); no Ethoca/Verifi RDR auto-refund bank alerts (avoids $19-$30 alert fees and silent refunds); physical tracked-parcel goods only (excludes digital goods, services, and untracked mailers).
- **Price:** $129/mo flat (up to 1,500 monthly orders) and $249/mo flat (up to 10,000 monthly orders); $0 alert fees and 0% commission on won chargebacks ($1,548-$2,988/yr) (More for Less)
