# Problem Score — Multi-Portal Inventory Feed Pre-Validator, Silent-Drop Watchdog & Portal-ROI Ledger for Specialty Vehicle & Equipment Dealers

ID: `IDEA-4` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-11, SIG-12, SIG-13, SIG-14

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Inventory managers, marketing managers, and dealer principals at independent marine/yacht brokerages, RV dealers, heavy equipment dealers, and commercial truck dealers (25-500 active units in stock, no enterprise dealer-group IT team) paying $1,000-$5,000+/mo across two or more paid marketplaces (BoatWizard/BoatTrader/YachtWorld, TraderTraxx/RVTrader/EquipmentTrader, Sandhills/MachineryTrader/TruckPaper).
- **Problem:** Independent marine, RV, heavy equipment, and commercial truck dealers paying $1,000-$5,000+/mo across multiple paid marketplaces face silent inventory feed drops and misclassifications (taxonomy/HIN/VIN/price/status mapping errors, stalled FTP/cron jobs) in BoatWizard, TraderTraxx, and Sandhills, and lack an independent cross-portal unit-level cost-per-lead ledger to audit spend or push back on 75%-100% portal renewal hikes.
- **Trade-offs:** Avoids building a full DMS, website CMS, or CRM (unlike Lightspeed, Dealer Spike, YachtOne); avoids high-volume Cloudflare scraping by relying on outbound XML/CSV pre-validation, portal error-email ingestion, and ADF/XML lead-email attribution; excludes Facebook Marketplace Chrome posters, OEM franchise car dealer groups, and <20-unit single-portal hobbyist brokers.
- **Price:** $149/mo (up to 150 active units across 3 portals) or $249/mo (up to 500 units across 6 portals); month-to-month credit card, zero setup fee. (Less for Less)
