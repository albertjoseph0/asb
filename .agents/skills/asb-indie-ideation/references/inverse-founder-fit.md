## Why Work Backwards From the Idea?

Jason Cohen's `asb-who-me` and `asb-voters` normally run **forward**: *know yourself → distill your 1–2 voters → pick a market that fits you.*

That works great when you already have a finished self-portrait, but during early exploration it creates a chicken-and-egg trap: an `[UNVETTED]` founder profile penalizes `Eager (Identity)` across promising verticals, AND you often don't realize *what kind of founder* a specific business secretly demands until you're six months deep in a workflow you hate.

**Inverse Founder-Market Fit** flips the lens: given a vetted idea (its buyer, inciting events, pricing story, Rude Q&A threats, and 2nd/3rd-order operational realities), **work backwards to reverse-engineer the exact Founder Archetype that makes this business unfair to compete against** — plus the exact ramp-up curriculum, credibility assets, or co-founder profile needed to close the gap.

## Four Creative Ways to Use `founder/`

1. **Forward Filter (`founder/WHO-ME.md` + `VOTERS.md`):** Anchor domain selection, `Eager (Identity)`, and Rude Q&A scars probes to who you already are.
2. **Inverse Blueprint (`FOUNDER-FIT-BLUEPRINT.md` via `workflows/reverse_founder_fit.py`):** Automatically generated for every surviving idea (or run standalone on any idea). Reveals the ideal founder archetype, required Voters, daily-work "Personal Hell" anti-archetype, and 30-day ramp-up plan.
3. **Co-Founder Complement Spec:** Separates the *Builder Voter* from the *Domain/Distribution Voter* so a technical founder knows immediately whether to solo-build, ramp up for 30 days, or recruit a specific industry insider as a co-founder/advisor.
4. **Archetype Comparison Lenses (`founder/archetypes/`):** Test the same industry through different reusable indie founder archetypes (e.g., *Open-Source DevTools Craftsperson* vs. *Ex-Agency Ops Automator* vs. *Media/Distribution-First Founder*) to see how changing the founder shifts `Eager (Identity)` and `Eager (Comparative)`.

## The 6-Part `FOUNDER-FIT-BLUEPRINT.md` Spec

Every generated blueprint answers six concrete, non-generic questions:

1. **Ideal Founder Archetype ("Who Wins This Easily?")** — Specific past career roles, lived workflows, and natural drives (`asb-who-me` style non-judgmental facts) that turn this business's hardest weekly chore into energizing work.
2. **The 1–2 Required Voters (`V1`, optional `V2`)** — Applying `asb-voters` in reverse: what idiosyncratic extreme trait overpowers this idea's indie weaknesses (e.g., extreme obsessiveness on latency/UX, insider domain fluency, or existing community trust)?
3. **Founder Personal Hell (Anti-Archetype — "Who Burns Out in 6 Months?")** — The exact daily/weekly recurring work this business actually forces on Month 6 (derived from Dalio's 2nd-order consequences!) so you can walk away fast if it hits your hindrances.
4. **30-Day Ramp-Up Curriculum ("Things to Know Before Talking to Buyers")** — Verified industry vocabulary, podcasts, trade associations, regulatory/platform docs, and competitor demos (`search_web` cited) that take an intelligent outsider from naive to credible in 30 days.
5. **Credibility & Co-Founder Bridge (`Eager-Identity: 0.1 → 0.5 → 1.0`)** — Concrete structural ways to earn trust without faking pedigree: design-partner councils, open-source / zero-custody architecture, public teardowns, or an exact Co-Founder / Advisor Complement profile (where to find them).
6. **5-Minute Founder-Fit Self-Mirror** — 6 binary yes/no diagnostic questions (zero platitudes) that let the human founder score their own fit against this idea immediately: *Go Solo*, *30-Day Ramp*, *Recruit Domain Co-Founder*, or *Pass*.

## Bridging `Eager (Identity)` From `0.1` → `0.5` → `1.0`

- **If `Eager-Identity` is already `0.5` (indie-friendly buyers):** The blueprint focuses on moving from `0.5 → 1.0` (mission/craft pull + insider vocabulary so Carol feels *"this person actually lived my week"*).
- **If `Eager-Identity` is `0.1` (trust-gated buyers):** The blueprint distinguishes *fixable* credibility gaps (30-day ramp + 3 named design partners + transparent architecture) from *structural* ones (needs licensed co-founder or E&O backing → usually a Pass for solo indie).
