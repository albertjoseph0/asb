# Problem Score — Regional 3PL Rate-Card Variance & 30-Day Dispute Auditor for Shopify Brands

ID: `IDEA-1` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-5, SIG-6

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Operations Managers, Supply Chain Leads, or Finance Controllers at $2M-$25M GMV Shopify DTC and CPG brands shipping 1,500-15,000 orders/month through outsourced 3PL warehouses running Extensiv (3PL Central), ShipHero, Deposco, or ShipBob WMS.
- **Problem:** Brands treat dense multi-thousand-line 3PL invoices like utility bills and auto-pay them under strict 30-day dispute clauses (such as ShipBob Section 5.7), permanently forfeiting 3%-10% in monthly overcharges from unamended pick/pack rate drift, DIM divisor mismatches (139 vs. 166), Day-1 peak-vs-average pallet storage traps, duplicate return handling, and ghost recurring account fees — while enterprise audit suites (Implentio $1,000-$2,000+/mo, Octup $2,500/mo) price them out and contingency agencies take 20%-25% of recovered cash forever.
- **Trade-offs:** Read-only variance auditing and dispute-packet export only (no write-back to Shopify, QuickBooks/NetSuite, or 3PL WMS); ingests structured billing CSV exports from Extensiv (3PL Central), ShipHero, Deposco, and ShipBob only (refuses scanned PDFs, EDI 210 freight bills, and LTL/ocean brokerage audits); flat monthly subscription with zero percentage-of-recovery commission and no phone negotiation with warehouse reps; audits contracted 3PL fulfillment, storage, and DIM-weight billing lines only (no UPS/FedEx parcel contract renegotiation or carrier damage claims).
- **Price:** $249/mo flat (up to 15,000 orders/mo and 2 warehouses; $2,490/yr annual) (Less for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** Finance Controllers and VP/Directors of Supply Chain at $10M-$50M GMV Shopify Plus CPG, Beverage, and Supplement brands shipping 10,000-50,000 orders/month across 2+ independent regional 3PLs running Extensiv (3PL Central) or Deposco WMS (excluding ShipBob and single-warehouse brands).
- **Problem:** Multi-node $10M-$50M CPG, Beverage, and Supplement brands spending $60k-$300k/mo across 2+ independent regional Extensiv or Deposco 3PLs suffer continuous monthly billing drift across divergent rate cards (lot/FEFO handling, cold-chain/pallet storage rules, bundle kitting, and retail-vs-DTC split fees) while Finance Controllers must close landed-COGS and dispute accruals into QuickBooks/NetSuite within 5 business days of month-end before 30-day forfeiture clauses expire — facing $1,500-$3,000/mo multi-module costs at Implentio.
- **Trade-offs:** Zero-PII CSV/SFTP ingestion only (order-ID and SKU-count exports; no Shopify customer PII or live ERP API write-back); deep specialization in multi-warehouse Extensiv (3PL Central) and Deposco CPG/lot/kitting/storage billing schemas plus monthly QBO/NetSuite landed-COGS and dispute-accrual CSV journal export (explicitly drops ShipBob and single-invoice spot checks); flat $799/mo subscription with 0% recovery commission and no manual warehouse phone negotiation; audits 3PL warehouse and fulfillment lines only (excludes UPS/FedEx parcel carrier contract renegotiation, least-cost carrier routing, and EDI 210 freight brokerage audits).
- **Price:** $799/mo flat (up to 4 warehouses and 50,000 orders/mo; $7,990/yr annual) (Less for Less)
