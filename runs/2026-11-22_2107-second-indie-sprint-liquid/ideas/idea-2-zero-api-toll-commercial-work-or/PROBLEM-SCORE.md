# Problem Score — Zero-API-Toll Commercial Work-Order Intake, Field-Proof & On-Site NTE Closeout Desk for QuickBooks/Jobber Trade Shops

ID: `IDEA-2` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-1, SIG-2, SIG-3, SIG-4, SIG-5

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Owners, dispatchers, and billing coordinators at 2-to-25-technician US commercial HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing, electrical, kitchen-equipment, and door/dock repair contractors running QuickBooks Online, Jobber, or Housecall Pro internally while taking recurring work orders across 3-8 client CMMS portals (ServiceChannel, CorrigoPro, Officetrax, Verisae, FMX).
- **Problem:** 2-to-25-tech commercial trade shops running QuickBooks Online, Jobber, or Housecall Pro spend heavy admin overhead manually re-keying work orders, NTE limits, POs, and invoices across 3-8 client CMMS portals (ServiceChannel, CorrigoPro, Officetrax, Verisae, FMX), while suffering 30-60 day invoice rejections, Contractor Scorecard downgrades from missing field documentation/check-ins, and unpaid return trips waiting on NTE increase approvals, yet are priced out of ServiceTitan/BuildOps rip-and-replace suites and $500/mo provider API contracts.
- **Trade-offs:** Deliberately excludes internal FSM dispatch/routing/inventory/payroll (keeping Jobber/HCP/QBO), refuses official portal REST APIs ($500/mo ServiceChannel API / CorrigoPro Direct SOW) in favor of inbound email parsing and SMS/web closeout packets, never spoofs mandatory portal GPS/IVR check-ins, and excludes residential shops, AIA GCs, and 50+ tech contractors.
- **Price:** $149/mo (up to 6 technicians / 120 portal work orders/mo) or $249/mo (up to 20 technicians / 400 work orders/mo); month-to-month credit card, zero setup fee. (Less for Less)
