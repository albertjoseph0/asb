- **Root Cause:** Two compounding market-structure errors: (1) Host-meter arbitrage — building an app whose sole economic wedge is exploiting Atlassian's Forge compute subsidy ($0.000025/GB-sec) to dodge Atlassian's own $0.50/1k Automation PAYG billing puts the business directly in the landlord's anti-circumvention crosshairs and inside Forge's hard 5-scheduled-trigger / 5-minute manifest caps; and (2) PAYG break-even upmarket drift — because Atlassian's pooled org-wide allowances (500k–2M/mo) and $0.50/1k PAYG make native overages cheaper below 500 seats, the idea was forced into 500–2,000+ seat Enterprise orgs where Atlassian's entire global >$10k Cloud ARR base is only 57,334 orgs (yielding <=1,000 qualifying accounts without ScriptRunner/JMWE), annual co-termed Marketplace contracts pin Liquid = 0.1, and a zero-egress Forge badge cannot satisfy Enterprise AEC certification, SLAs, legal anti-circumvention review, or solo bus-factor gates (Eager-Identity = 0.1).

## 4. Candidate Learned Principles (Promote to `principles/LEARNED-PRINCIPLES.md`)

- **Audit Public Marketplace Install Telemetry & Ecosystem Ceilings Before Synthesis:** Before synthesizing any B2B app-marketplace concept (Atlassian Marketplace, Microsoft AppSource, HubSpot, Salesforce), verify two hard empirical ceilings in Phase 1: (a) the host ecosystem's paid mid-market customer count (which caps post-filter Plausible), and (b) live public install counts of the top 3–5 adjacent leaders plus recent 12-month entrants. If category leaders show <2% ecosystem penetration and recent entrants sit at single-digit (1–10) installs, assign Self-Aware = 0.1 and auto-kill — never mistake partner/consultant blog evangelism for revealed buyer pull. *(Evidence: SectionGuard for Confluence echoed Atlassian partner hype around 'Rovo AI Readiness,' yet live Marketplace telemetry proved the #1 archiving leader (Midori) holds only ~1,100 installs (<2% of paid Confluence orgs) and every 2025–2026 Forge hygiene/AI-readiness app (CodeTailor, Evergreen, Page Hygiene Report, Stale Page Finder, Doc Ownership & Reviews) sits at 1–3 installs inside Atlassian's 57,334-customer >$10k Cloud ARR ceiling.)*
- **Reject 'Zero-Egress Badge' Trust Illusions & Site-Wide Matched-Tier Licensing Traps:** Never treat platform-hosted zero-egress runtimes ('Runs on Atlassian' Forge, SharePoint-resident SPFx) as a shortcut to Eager-Identity >= 0.5 or Liquid = 1.0 for an unvetted indie founder. Zero-egress addresses data residency only; enterprise IT still demands vendor-level certifications (Atlassian Enterprise Certified, Microsoft 365 Level 3 Certification), Entra ID tenant-wide write Admin Consent audits, ISO 27001 supplier vetting, SLAs, and zero solo bus-factor (Eager-Identity = 0.1). Simultaneously, Matched-Tier site-wide or tenant-wide annual co-termed billing taxes non-target seats and pushes purchase into annual IT renewals (Liquid = 0.1), where ecosystem-bounded Plausible (<=10k) fails the DP16 $100M floor. Only enter B2B ecosystems that permit team- or usage-scoped self-serve billing without tenant-wide Admin Consent. *(Evidence: All three marketplace candidates relied on zero-egress host residency yet suffered Liquid = 0.1 from site-wide Matched-Tier / co-termed annual IT billing (SectionGuard forced 70% non-dev seat licensing; StepShield required $9k–$18k/yr co-termed enterprise contracts), while StepShield and SOPVault 365 both hit Eager-Identity = 0.1 due to AEC/M365 Level 3 certification, Entra write-scope Admin Consent, ISO 27001 A.5.19 supplier review, and solo bus-factor objections.)*
- **Ban Host-Meter Arbitrage Plays (Never Undercut the Landlord on the Landlord's Subsidized Compute):** Auto-disqualify any marketplace app whose primary ROI is exploiting a platform's subsidized compute or runtime tier to bypass or undercut the host platform's own usage-based monetization. Unlike passive feature absorption (DP11), host-meter arbitrage creates direct financial conflict with the sole platform landlord — exposing the product to Developer Terms anti-circumvention enforcement, manifest/quota throttles, and abrupt pricing revocations — while the host's native PAYG break-even floor pushes target buyers upmarket into high-friction Enterprise accounts (Plausible <=1,000, Eager-Identity = 0.1). *(Evidence: StepShield for Jira attempted to arbitrage Atlassian's $0.000025/GB-sec Forge compute subsidy against Atlassian's own $0.50/1k Automation PAYG overages (effective Dec 3, 2026), triggering direct Developer Terms anti-circumvention exposure, Forge's 5-scheduled-trigger / 5-minute manifest caps, and a native PAYG break-even threshold that forced an unviable pivot into <=1,000 500–2,000+ seat Enterprise orgs (Score = 0.04).)*
- **Timestamp-Gate EOL Inciting Events & Audit Seat-Basis Pricing Inversion Across Target Bands:** Validate every EOL, deprecation, or billing-change inciting event against the current run date AND the buyer's procurement cycle: auto-reject elapsed cutoffs and <90-day deadlines inside Liquid = 0.1 IT environments where replacements are already locked. Separately, audit pricing-metric parity across the full target seat range (e.g., 50 / 150 / 250 seats) before claiming 'More for Less' — never apply per-tenant-seat pricing to broad-readership, low-frequency workflows where incumbents offer flat per-site or unlimited-user pricing. *(Evidence: SOPVault 365 anchored urgency on Microsoft's April 2, 2026 SharePoint 2013 workflow hard retirement ~8 months after the deadline had already passed (run date Nov 2026), and priced passive SOP attestation at $3/user/mo ($5,400–$7,500/yr at 150–250 seats) — inverting its 'More for Less' claim by costing more than fuller-featured flat/unlimited incumbents Xoralia (~$4,000/yr) and qmsWrapper (€3,499/yr) while delivering fewer features.)*
