## Seed Principles (ASB + Dalio Foundation)

- **DP1 — Separate Proximate Symptom from Root Cause.** When an idea fails Problem Score or Rude Q&A, never stop at "score was 0.2." Diagnose *why* the scouting or synthesis step generated a non-viable target so future runs avoid that entire class of trap.
- **DP2 — Weight Evidence by Believability, Never by Volume of Words.** One live pricing page or three independent firsthand buyer complaints (`0.7–1.0`) outweigh ten paragraphs of LLM Fermi optimism (`0.2`).
- **DP3 — Reject First-Order Seduction.** Never pick an idea solely because "people complain Tool X is too expensive." Always trace 2nd-order support/churn economics and 3rd-order platform/incumbent responses before shortlisting.
- **DP4 — Demand an Indie-Compatible Root Cause for Why the Pain Persists.** A real problem that hasn't been solved cleanly is only an indie opportunity if incumbents ignore it for structural reasons (too small for VC ACV, business-model conflict, niche vertical specialization) — NOT because buyers refuse to pay or cannot switch.
- **DP5 — Pre-Commit Kill Triggers Before Attachment Sets In.** Every surviving idea enters customer discovery with an explicit, falsifiable walk-away threshold tested in 10–15 real conversations before writing application code.
- **DP6 — Surface Disagreement; Never Average It Away.** When high-believability specialists disagree (e.g., strong Problem Score vs. severe 2nd-order consequence risk), flag the split explicitly and make that exact crux Goal #1 of customer interviews.
- **DP7 — Quarantine Synthetic Pre-Tests & Cap Believability at `0.35`.** Blind, web-grounded, anti-sycophantic synthetic persona interviews are a cheap stress-test to catch leading questions, status-quo inertia, and anti-market leaks — never a substitute for real human validation (`1.0`). They live strictly in `synthetic-interviews/` and can flag red lights or tighten scripts, never mark a hypothesis `✓ VALIDATED`.

## Active Empirical Principles (Earned Across 35 Live Runs)

- **DP10 — Avoid Catastrophic-Liability & Institutional-Trust Walls for Unvetted Founders.** Disqualify ideas where software failure creates `$1M+` strict legal liability, statutory PII/PHI/IRS/HIPAA exposure, enterprise SOC 2 / Entra tenant-wide Admin Consent, or AEC/M365 Level 3 certification gates whenever the founder profile is unvetted (`Eager-Identity = 0.1`).
- **DP12 — Ban ToS-Violating Portal Automation & Host-Free-Import Redundancy.** Auto-kill any browser-extension/scraping automation that violates host Web App Terms of Service and risks customer account suspension, or duplicates native free bulk-import utilities.
- **DP17 — Target Large Proven Categories (`100k–10M`) With an Incumbent-Uncopyable Structural Wedge.** Follow Jason Cohen's canonical ASB winners (`WP Engine = 4.0`, `ConvertKit = 2.0`): avoid thin 3-tool intersectional bolt-ons (`Plausible = 1k–10k`). Enter large, already-budgeted self-serve categories (`Plausible = 100k–10M`, `Lucrative = $100–$1k/yr`) with a core operational product (`Enduring = 0.5–1.0`) and win `Eager (Comparative) = 0.5` through a structural trade-off incumbents *refuse* to copy (e.g., BYO-infrastructure zero-markup vs. bandwidth/usage arbitrage, flat workspace vs. per-seat/commission tax, or opinionated vertical/segment focus vs. horizontal bloat).
- **DP26 — Verify Competitor Disqualifiers on Primary Docs & Prefer Single-Buyer Core Products.** Never claim a competitor lacks a feature without checking live 2026 primary pricing/docs, non-SaaS floors (desktop/App Store/OSS), and native platform launches. Prefer standalone single-buyer products where the buyer owns 100% of input data over disconnected sidecars that force double-entry.
- **DP30 — Gate Copyrighted Third-Party Data Moats & 18–36 Month Solo Engineering Tarpits.** Reject ideas whose credible outputs require IP-locked reference catalogs (botanical/SKU databases) or 18–36 month 3-in-1 solo engineering engines (custom CAD + native iPadOS, CoreMedia NLE) — favor focused indie v1s shippable in 6–12 weeks.
- **DP31 — Audit Live Marketplace Install Telemetry & Ecosystem Ceilings.** Before proposing any B2B app-store plugin (Atlassian, AppSource, HubSpot), check host paid-org ceilings and live install counts of top leaders + recent 12-month entrants; do not confuse partner blog buzz with buyer pull.
- **DP33 — Ban Host-Meter Arbitrage Plays.** Auto-disqualify any marketplace app whose primary ROI is exploiting a host platform's subsidized compute to undercut the host's own usage-metering fees (direct anti-circumvention risk).

## Truth-Calibration & Anti-Cynicism Principles (Earned From 35-Kill Audit)

- **DP35 — Score `Plausible` at True Fermi Problem-Holder Scale (Never Double-Count `Liquid`, `Self-Aware`, or `Eager` Inside `Plausible`).** *(Promoted from 35-run calibration audit.)* Per `asb-problem`, `Plausible` is the coarse power-of-10 count of specific entities who **have the problem** (anchors: `100M` WordPress sites for WP Engine, `10M` creators for ConvertKit) — anchored bottom-up from real ecosystem/software-buyer counts (not raw BLS umbrellas), and **NEVER** shrunk by stacking switching-timing (`Liquid`), awareness (`Self-Aware`), or competitor-preference (`Eager`) filters inside `Plausible`. Each of those already multiplies separately in the formula; filtering them inside `Plausible` double-counts penalties by 10x–100x.
- **DP36 — Calibrate `Eager (Comparative) = 0.5` Faithfully to `WP Engine` & `ConvertKit` (No Manufactured Cynicism).** *(Promoted from 35-run calibration audit.)* Per `asb-problem` ("No curve — in either direction"): `0.1` is for true undifferentiated clones where a free/cheap rival already solves the exact core workflow for the exact same buyer without the incumbent's structural pain. Award `0.5` plainly whenever the product delivers a real, incumbent-uncopyable business-model or architectural trade-off that a clear sub-segment would buy for alone — **even when** `$0–$49/mo` generalists, raw developer APIs/OSS scripts, or single-ecosystem plugins exist (just as WP Engine earned `0.5` vs. `$3/mo` hosts and ConvertKit earned `0.5` vs. free Mailchimp & `$19/mo` AWeber). Requiring "zero cheap rivals on Earth" conflates `0.5` with `1.0`.
- **DP37 — Keep `Eager (Identity) = 0.5` as the Baseline for Self-Serve Credit-Card SaaS.** *(Promoted from 35-run calibration audit.)* Per `asb-problem`, `0.5` means *"indifferent, no red flags"* — the honest default for an indie founder selling self-serve credit-card software to individuals, creators, freelancers, small agencies, developers, or SMB owners, especially when customer data/infrastructure is portable or customer-owned (eliminating solo bus-factor lock-in). Reserve `0.1` strictly for genuine institutional trust walls (`DP10`).

- **DP11, DP13, DP15, DP18, DP21, DP24, DP27, DP34** — *[CONSOLIDATED into DP17 & DP26]*: Account honestly for switching rails, seasonal freezes, VA benchmarks, and pricing-metric parity inside `Liquid`, `Lucrative`, and `Enduring` (using `Liquid = 0.1` honestly when annual renewal cycles apply, rather than forcing fake `1.0` or rejecting viable `100k–10M` markets that clear `>= 1.0` at `Liquid = 0.1`).
