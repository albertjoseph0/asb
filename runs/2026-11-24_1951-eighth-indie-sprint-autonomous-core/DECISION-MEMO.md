### BranchSim Studio: Mac/Browser Interactive Branching, One-Pass Software Simulation & Open-SCORM Authoring
- **Proximate Cause (Symptom):** Killed in Phase 3 with Problem Score = 0.002 (EBI = 0.70, Min = 0.40) across four failed multipliers: Eager-Comparative = 0.1 (ActivePresenter 10 Pro at $499 perpetual native Mac, Adobe Captivate at $34-$40/mo with live cloud Review links, FinchLearn at $29-$49/mo open .ocf with .story import, and Recourse/LMSable already cover the claimed wedge), Plausible = 10,000 (~19k-25k post-filter global buying units), Liquid = 0.1 (buyers locked into annual Articulate/Captivate/iSpring contracts and template inertia), and Lucrative = 100 ($49/mo Less-for-Less with pause-anytime offline editing caps realistic net ARPU at ~$250-$468/yr).
- **Root Cause:** Three compounding Phase 0-2 errors: (1) Macro-occupation TAM inflation combined with an OS-lockout slice—Phase 0 equated 472.5k BLS classroom trainers with SCORM-authoring buyers instead of anchoring to Articulate's ~125k customer organizations (~300k global authoring entities), then stacked four filters (Mac + solo/1-5 + branching/sim + non-.story) that shrank TAM by >90%. (2) Non-SaaS and changelog blindness—scouting relied on stale forum grievances against Articulate/Captivate while missing perpetual-license native Mac incumbents (ActivePresenter 10 Pro) and live Captivate cloud Review docs. (3) Output-portability liquidity fallacy paired with self-cannibalizing pricing—equating standalone SCORM ZIP delivery with Liquid = 1.0 ignored annual competitor contract lock-in and creation-side template inertia, while allowing free offline editing during paused months guaranteed project-burst churn and $100-bucket ARPU.
- **Machine Fix for Future Runs:** In Phase 0/1: (1) Compute Plausible bottom-up from disclosed incumbent customer counts divided by market share rather than BLS occupational headcount, and auto-reject OS-filtered ('Mac-only') wedges that fall below 100,000 post-filter buyers (or 10,000 at >=$1,000/yr). (2) Mandate primary-doc checks of perpetual-license native desktop tools (e.g., ActivePresenter) and current help/changelog docs of named incumbents (e.g., Captivate Review links) before Phase 2. (3) Require a creation-side liquidity test (annual license cycles + authoring template inertia) independent of export format portability, and ban self-cannibalizing pause-anytime offline editing on Less-for-Less solo tiers.

## 4. Candidate Learned Principles (Promote to `principles/LEARNED-PRINCIPLES.md`)

- **Separate Output Portability From Creation-Side Switching Liquidity (The Authoring/CAD Liquid = 0.1 Trap):** Never assign Liquid = 1.0 solely because a standalone tool outputs portable deliverables (SCORM ZIPs, PDFs, PNGs) with zero downstream LMS/CRM migration. Audit creation-side switching locks upfront: (1) upfront annual incumbent licenses (the exact annual bill your pitch targets traps ~90% of buyers mid-contract), (2) accumulated creation assets (templates, symbol libraries, pricing catalogs), and (3) operator muscle-memory retraining deferred to each buyer's personal off-season—recognizing that averaging across climates/hemispheres does not convert account-level Liquid = 0.1 into 1.0. *(Evidence: Both BranchSim Studio (portable SCORM ZIP output, Problem Score 0.002) and CanopyBid (PDF planting proposals, Problem Score 0.1) were assigned Liquid = 1.0 in Phase 0 for avoiding SOR migration, then collapsed to Liquid = 0.1 in Phase 3 due to annual Articulate/CAD contract lock-in, template/symbol/pricing-catalog inertia, and individual off-season tool-refresh windows.)*
- **Anchor TAM Bottom-Up From Software-Buying Entities Before Applying OS/Hardware Filters:** Forbid top-down Plausible estimates built from raw BLS occupational headcounts or broad NAICS umbrellas where <5% of entities buy the specialty software. Anchor Phase 0 Plausible bottom-up from disclosed incumbent customer counts divided by market share (or Census sub-activity revenue shares), and verify that any OS/hardware wedge ('Mac/iPad-native escape from Windows legacy') still clears the DP16 $10M floor after applying the ~65% OS slice and behavioral filters. *(Evidence: BranchSim Studio anchored on 472.5k BLS training specialists rather than ~300k global SCORM-authoring buying entities (Articulate's 125k orgs at ~40-45% share), collapsing after Mac/solo/branching/non-.story filters to ~19k-25k (Plausible = 10,000); CanopyBid cited 696k NAICS 561730 landscaping firms despite <2% Census design receipts, collapsing after Mac/iPad 2D planting filters to ~15k-35k (Plausible = 10,000).)*
- **Audit Non-SaaS Floors (Perpetual Desktop & App Store Pricing) and True Duct-Tape Stack Spend:** Extend Phase 1 floor sweeps beyond web SaaS and OSS (DP8/DP20/DP23) to verify (1) native perpetual-license desktop software, (2) Apple/Mac/iPad App Store utilities at live checkout prices, and (3) job-output artifact synonyms (e.g., 'sell sheet', 'line sheet', 'food broker software'). Calculate the buyer's true annual spend on their existing duct-tape stack before claiming 'Less for Less', and ban 'pause-anytime + full offline editing' terms that structurally compress solo net ARPU into the $100/yr bucket. *(Evidence: BranchSim Studio missed ActivePresenter 10 Pro ($499 perpetual native Mac), Adobe Captivate's live cloud Review links, and self-cannibalized $49/mo ARPU via pause-anytime offline editing (Lucrative = 100, Eager-Comparative = 0.1); CanopyBid missed Sow Pro+ ($99.99/yr) and mispriced Morpholio Trace Pro at $20-$24/mo instead of ~$25/yr on the App Store, pricing $588/yr at 2.1x the actual ~$275/yr duct-tape stack; ShelfPitch searched 'planogram software' and missed Shelf Scout, Satellite CPG, Brandboom, and $0-tier OPENCatman.)*
- **Gate Hidden Third-Party Reference-Data Moats and the Episodic-SMB vs. Tiny-Agency Split:** Before claiming 'buyer owns 100% of input data' (DP26), verify whether credible deliverables actually depend on shared third-party reference datasets (competitor SKU dimensions/packshots, syndicated POS velocity, regional market comps)—if incumbents' moat is a proprietary multi-party database, reject a BYO-upload UI studio. Simultaneously reject lenses caught in the Episodic-SMB vs. Tiny-Agency Split, where the 100k+ SMB pool performs the job 1-2x/yr (causing export-and-cancel churn) while weekly-use agency/broker intermediaries form a ~1,000-entity pool lacking $10k/yr standalone software budgets. *(Evidence: ShelfPitch failed Scenario A on single-brand emerging CPG annual-reset export-and-cancel churn, then pivoted in Scenario B to ~1,000 boutique CPG brokerages (Plausible = 1,000, Problem Score 0.02), where buyer-upload-only shelf visuals lost to OPENCatman's multi-million-SKU competitor library and Satellite CPG's SPINS IX-ONE/UNFI sync, while broker $15k-$100k+ budgets went to syndicated scan data rather than $2,490/yr pitch studios (Lucrative = 1,000).)*
