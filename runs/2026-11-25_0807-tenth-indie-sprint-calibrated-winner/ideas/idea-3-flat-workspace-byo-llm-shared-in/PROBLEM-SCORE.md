# Problem Score — Flat-Workspace + BYO-LLM Shared Inbox for SaaS & Shopify Stores (Lens 3)

ID: `IDEA-3` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-9

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Self-serve SaaS companies and Shopify/WooCommerce DTC e-commerce stores (2-20 staff, handling 500-10,000+ monthly customer support email and chat conversations) currently paying $200-$1,500+/mo on Intercom, Help Scout, Gorgias, or Freshdesk.
- **Problem:** SaaS founders and DTC e-commerce merchants face compounding seat, ticket-volume, and per-resolution AI taxes on modern helpdesks — Intercom ($29-$132/seat/mo + $0.99/Fin outcome), Help Scout ($25-$75/seat/mo + $0.75/Envoy AI resolution), and Gorgias ($60-$1,430/mo ticket tiers + $0.90-$1.50/AI interactions that also consume base ticket quotas) — while mainstream sub-$100 rivals (Crisp, BoldDesk, Re:amaze, Tidio) cap seats/conversations, meter AI credits, or omit native Shopify/Stripe refund/cancel actions, and self-hosted FreeScout requires stitching modules and remains read-only for Shopify/Stripe.
- **Trade-offs:** 100% BYO-LLM API keys (OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini) with zero vendor token resale or per-resolution AI fees; flat $59/mo unlimited seats and unlimited email/chat tickets; draft-first AI guardrails (auto-send strictly opt-in); focused strictly on email + lightweight web widget + native 1-click Shopify/Stripe order actions while deliberately conceding outbound marketing campaigns, product tours, voice/IVR telephony, omnichannel WhatsApp/social suites, and enterprise HIPAA/SOC 2/Entra SSO governance.
- **Price:** $59/mo flat workspace ($590/yr annual) — unlimited human agents, unlimited email/chat tickets, native Shopify & Stripe action sidebar, and 100% BYO OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini API keys at raw token cost. (Less for Less)
