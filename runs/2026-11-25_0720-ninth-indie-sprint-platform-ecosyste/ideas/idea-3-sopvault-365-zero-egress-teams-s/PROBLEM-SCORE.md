# Problem Score — SOPVault 365 (Zero-Egress Teams & SharePoint ISO Document Control)

ID: `IDEA-3` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-12

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Quality Managers, Compliance Officers, and M365 IT Administrators at 50-250 seat ISO 9001 or ISO 27001 certified manufacturers, engineering firms, and regulated B2B service companies standardized on Microsoft 365 whose SharePoint 2013 approval workflows broke on April 2, 2026 or whose DIY Power Automate flows fail version-locked policy attestations.
- **Problem:** Broken SharePoint 2013 SOP approval workflows (retired April 2, 2026) and brittle DIY Power Automate flows that fail to invalidate prior employee acknowledgments across SOP versions, lack automated Draft/Published/Obsolete permission separation, and produce no one-click ISO 9001 Clause 7.5 / ISO 27001 A.5.1 audit evidence packs.
- **Trade-offs:** 100% SharePoint/M365 tenant-resident storage (zero multi-cloud/Google Workspace support); strictly ISO 9001 Clause 7.5 and ISO 27001 A.5.1 controlled-document lifecycles and Teams read attestations (excluding CAPA, calibration, supplier audits, FDA 21 CFR Part 11 biometric signatures, and consulting); opinionated Draft -> Review/Approve -> Read-Only Published -> Obsolete Archive transitions (no custom workflow canvas builder).
- **Price:** $3.00/user/month self-serve transactable via Microsoft AppSource / Teams Admin Center (3% Microsoft commission; $1,800/yr at 50 seats; $3,600/yr at 100 seats; $7,500/yr at 250 seats tiered; 14-day free trial billed on existing M365 invoice). (More for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** Quality Managers and Document Controllers at 50-250 seat discrete ISO 9001 manufacturers (CNC machining, metal fabrication, electronics assembly, packaging) standardized on Microsoft 365 SharePoint Online who maintain printed/laminated SOP binders and work-instruction sheets at physical shop-floor workstations.
- **Problem:** Discrete ISO 9001 manufacturers managing SOPs in SharePoint Online suffer recurring Clause 7.5.3.2(d) surveillance-audit non-conformities when printed/laminated workstation copies silently go obsolete after a SharePoint revision. Digital-attestation add-ons (Compliance Tracker 365, DocRead, Xoralia) do not control physical floor printouts, forcing plants to either wire brittle Power Automate flows to $300-$500/mo PDF engines (Muhimbi/Plumsail) plus custom QR scripts or pay $15k-$50k+/yr to migrate out of SharePoint into full eQMS/work-instruction suites.
- **Trade-offs:** Strictly SharePoint-native controlled-copy PDF watermarking and live QR-code obsolete-version gating for physical manufacturing floors (dropping ISO 27001 IT-policy Teams Bots, CAPA, calibration, and external SOP migration); flat $199/mo per-tenant pricing for unlimited shop-floor scanners (eliminating the per-seat reader penalty for non-desk workers); pure SPFx .sppkg with zero SOP file egress (QR lookup stores only opaque doc-ID/revision-hash validity flags so shared floor tablets need no Entra ID MFA logins).
- **Price:** $199/month ($2,388/year billed annually) flat per Microsoft 365 tenant — unlimited shop-floor scanners and readers (AppSource transactable, 14-day free trial; eliminates per-seat reader penalty for frontline manufacturing staff). (More for Less)
