## Root-Cause Persistence
- The pain persists due to a split root cause — only half of which is structurally favorable, while the decisive half is hostile to a $499/mo indie read-only SaaS: (1) Real platform/marketplace fragmentation (verified via Meta/TikTok/UsageHawk/Archive sources): Meta Partnership Ads separate indefinite technical permissions from private 30/90/180-day legal contracts (no native expiry cutoff), TikTok Spark Ads enforce hard 7/30/60/365-day auth_code cutoffs (error 4025 / 900010) with no auto-renew or proactive Slack alerts, and walled-garden marketplaces (Insense, Cohley, Vidovo) resist managing off-platform/agency creators because their revenue depends on keeping sourcing on-platform. (2) Non-indie-favorable root causes (decisive against Scenario B): First, on Meta Ads, a large share of DTC UGC runs as brand-handle dark posts continuously remixed into 10-30 hook variants, mashups, and captioned edits — every edit creates a brand-new Meta Post ID / video_id with zero creator metadata exposed by ads_read, meaning the problem persists because of messy human creative-lineage tracking that read-only Ads APIs cannot cleanly solve without manual naming discipline (Airtable parity) or brittle computer-vision/audio fingerprinting. Second, brands cheaply mitigate much of the pain structurally via 365-day Spark codes upfront, perpetual buyouts on commodity UGC (since 80%+ of paid social ads fatigue inside 45-90 days), $0-$30/mo Airtable/Notion + Zapier Slack alerts, or $49-$129/mo ugcrightstracker.com. Third, horizontal incumbents already inside 8-figure DTC stacks with live Meta/TikTok OAuth tokens (Archive — which already tracks DM usage rights and Spark/Partnership expirations — plus Motion $999/mo, Foreplay, Atria, Air.inc, and TikTok suites Cruva/Reacher/Euka) have ZERO business-model conflict preventing them from shipping read-only expiry alerts as a 2-week feature. *(Indie-favorable: `False`)*
