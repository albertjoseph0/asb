# Problem Score — BranchSim Studio: Mac/Browser Interactive Branching, One-Pass Software Simulation & Open-SCORM Authoring

ID: `IDEA-1` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-1, SIG-2, SIG-3, SIG-4

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Mac-using independent instructional designers, freelance L&D consultants, and 1-5 person corporate, SaaS customer-education, or higher-ed training teams who deliver working SCORM 1.2/2004/xAPI modules (into Moodle, Canvas, TalentLMS, Docebo, or Absorb) on contracts that do NOT legally mandate Articulate .story source-file handoff.
- **Problem:** Mac-using independent instructional designers and 1-5 person training teams delivering SCORM 1.2/2004/xAPI interactive branching scenarios and clickable software simulations face Articulate 360's $1,449-$1,749/yr upfront subscription, Windows-only Storyline (requiring Parallels/Windows 11 ARM on Apple Silicon), cloud-locked Rise courses, and review/QA friction, while seeking a browser/Mac-native local-first studio with one-pass Demo/Try-Me/Test-Me software capture, visual node-graph branching, and zero-login review links with variable debugging.
- **Trade-offs:** No .story binary import/export (ceding Articulate-mandated enterprise subcontracts); no stock photo library, AI avatar studio, or PPT-to-slideware converter; local-first portable JSON + offline standalone SCORM/xAPI with zero Dynamic SCORM lock-in (editable even when paused); no built-in LMS hosting, learner PII database, or enterprise SSO/procurement; Chrome-extension web-app capture only (no native desktop app capture).
- **Price:** $49/mo solo (pause/cancel anytime; $468/yr optional annual) or $99/mo for 3-seat small training teams, with unlimited offline SCORM/xAPI exports and free stakeholder review links. (Less for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** 1-5 person B2B SaaS Customer Education and Product-Training teams at fast-shipping SaaS companies (operating academies on Skilljar, Thought Industries, WorkRamp, Docebo, Absorb, or TalentLMS) who must continuously maintain graded clickable software simulations (Demo / Practice / Certification Test-Me) across frequent web-app UI releases.
- **Problem:** Lean 1-5 person B2B SaaS Customer Education teams running academies on Skilljar, Thought Industries, WorkRamp, Docebo, Absorb, or TalentLMS face an unsustainable SCORM maintenance loop: bi-weekly web-app UI releases instantly break static screenshot simulations built in Storyline, Captivate, or ActivePresenter, forcing manual re-recording, hotspot redrawing, and LMS ZIP re-uploading—leaving customer onboarding and certification training perpetually behind product releases.
- **Trade-offs:** Drops solo Mac freelance IDs, broad general-purpose branching/compliance course authoring, native desktop-app capture (Excel/SAP GUI), and pause-anytime local-only pricing; focuses exclusively on web-based B2B SaaS products with repeatable staging environments and Playwright/semantic-DOM selector replay that auto-regenerates Demo/Practice/Graded Test-Me SCORM/xAPI modules when the UI changes.
- **Price:** $249/mo ($2,988/yr) flat team plan for up to 5 customer-education authors, unlimited DOM-replayed software simulations, automated staging UI-diff refreshes, and Live-SCORM/xAPI academy sync (no solo pause-anytime tier). (More for More)
