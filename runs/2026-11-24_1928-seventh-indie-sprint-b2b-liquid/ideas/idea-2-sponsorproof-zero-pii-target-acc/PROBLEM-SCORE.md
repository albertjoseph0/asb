# Problem Score — SponsorProof — Zero-PII Target-Account Overlap & 14-Day Sponsor ROI Audit Pack for B2B Conferences

ID: `IDEA-2` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-6, SIG-5, SIG-8

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Sponsorship directors and event operations managers at independent B2B conference producers, regional/vertical trade and professional associations, and B2B field-event teams running 2+ sponsored B2B conferences, summits, or executive workshops per year (selling $2k-$50k+ sponsorships) on Eventbrite, Luma, WildApricot, Swoogo, or Cvent/Bizzabo without migrating core ticketing or AMS systems.
- **Problem:** Under 2026 CFO budget scrutiny, B2B sponsors cut event attendance and reject vanity logo recaps in favor of ICP seniority match, Target-Account presence, and attributed leads; organizers scramble across spreadsheets, drives, and Canva to prove deliverables and defend renewals during the 14-day post-event window without migrating core ticketing or AMS systems.
- **Trade-offs:** Runs strictly as a per-event CSV sidecar (no ticketing, payments, badge printing, or attendee app) alongside Eventbrite/Luma/WildApricot/Swoogo/Cvent; client-side browser PII scrubbing ingesting only Company Domain, Job Title, Industry, and Ticket Tier for blind Target-Account overlap; B2B corporate sponsorship ROI only (excludes consumer festivals, food vendors, charity galas, and third-party event discovery databases).
- **Price:** $129/month flat per organizer (unlimited B2B events, up to 50 sponsors per event) or $1,290/year (More for More)
