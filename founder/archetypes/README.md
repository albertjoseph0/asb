# Reusable Indie Founder Archetype Lenses

Use these in two creative ways:
1. **Work Backwards (Inverse Mode — `workflows/reverse_founder_fit.py`):** Start from any vetted startup idea and generate `FOUNDER-FIT-BLUEPRINT.md` — revealing the Ideal Founder Archetype, Required Voters (`V1–V2`), Month-6 Personal Hell, 30-Day Ramp-Up Curriculum, Co-Founder Complement, and 6-Question Self-Fit Mirror.
2. **Work Forwards (Archetype Comparison Mode):** Copy or adapt one of the archetypes below into `founder/FOUNDER-PROFILE.md` to see how shifting the founder's Voter changes `Eager (Identity)`, `Eager (Comparative)`, and which ideas survive.

## 4 Canonical Indie Founder Archetypes

### 1. The Deep-Tech DevTools Craftsperson
- **Core Voter:** Obsessive engineering taste, performance/reliability craft, open-source credibility (`GitHub` / `HN` native).
- **Naturally Wins:** CLI/CI guardrails, dev infrastructure, schema/testing/observability tools where buyers *prefer* buying from a sharp solo builder (`Eager-Identity = 0.5–1.0`).
- **Personal Hell:** Cold-calling local business owners, chasing unpaid invoices by phone, managing non-technical support queues.

### 2. The Systems-Ops Workflow Automator
- **Core Voter:** Relentless simplification of messy multi-tool B2B workflows into opinionated, zero-bloat single-screen apps.
- **Naturally Wins:** Agency ops, merchant ops, boutique studio workflows where buyers drown in Notion/Airtable/Zapier duct tape (`More for Less`).
- **Personal Hell:** Pure algorithmic/research R&D with no weekly customer workflow contact, or multi-year enterprise RFPs.

### 3. The Distribution-First Creator / Educator Founder
- **Core Voter:** Builds audience trust via teardowns, templates, newsletters, or YouTube tutorials before writing a line of code.
- **Naturally Wins:** Prosumer/creator/consultant tools where organic content makes CAC near-zero and buyers buy *because it's from you* (`Eager-Identity = 1.0`).
- **Personal Hell:** Invisible backend infrastructure where content/community cannot reach the buyer.

### 4. The Technical Builder + Domain Insider Pair (Co-Founder Bridge)
- **Core Voter:** Technical velocity (`V1`) paired with an industry insider's pedigree, warm Rolodex, and vocabulary (`V2`).
- **Naturally Wins:** High-ACV vertical SaaS (`$299–$999/mo`) where an outsider alone gets pinned at `Eager-Identity = 0.1`, but an insider co-founder flips it to `0.5–1.0` overnight.
- **Personal Hell:** Solo cold outbound into skeptical, closed trade associations without an insider champion.
