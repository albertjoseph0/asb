# Problem Score — Zero-3PA ADF/XML Spec-Grounded Lead Qualifier & Freight/Trade-In Intake for Commercial Equipment, Truck & Trailer Dealers

ID: `IDEA-3` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-11, SIG-12, SIG-13, SIG-15

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Sales managers and dealer principals at independent U.S. commercial heavy equipment, semi-truck, vocational truck, and commercial trailer dealerships and machinery brokers (15–300 active units in stock, $25,000–$500,000+ inventory, no dedicated BDC desk) actively listing on MachineryTrader, TruckPaper, TractorHouse, EquipmentTrader, and CommercialTruckTrader.
- **Problem:** Independent commercial equipment, truck, and trailer dealers spend $1,000–$3,000+/mo on Sandhills and Trader Interactive listings, lose after-hours high-intent buyers to faster yards, and waste sales-rep hours on unscreened tire-kickers asking unit-specific spec, financing, trade-in, and out-of-state lowboy freight questions while vertical BDC suites and DMS 3PA connectors cost $399–$1,900+/mo.
- **Trade-offs:** Bypasses legacy DMS 3PA connectors (Lightspeed/CDK) by scraping public dealer VDP URLs and intercepting ADF/XML inquiry emails; excludes consumer auto, powersports, full website hosting, cold dialing, Voice AI, and Facebook Marketplace auto-posting bots; gives non-binding ballpark lowboy/step-deck mileage ranges rather than binding freight quotes or credit pulls.
- **Price:** $169/mo per yard (up to 400 qualified leads/mo, SMS included) or $299/mo multi-yard (up to 3 yards / 1,200 leads/mo); month-to-month credit card, zero DMS setup fee. (Less for Less)
