### BriefLock — Two-Sided Hiring-Manager Calibration & Feedback-SLA Charter Gate for Boutique Recruiting Agencies
- **Proximate Cause (Symptom):** Killed in Phase 3 Problem Score at 0.20 in both Scenario A (EBI = 0.70, Min = 0.40) and Scenario B (EBI = 0.657, Min = 0.40). Scenario A failed on Eager-Comparative = 0.1 and Lucrative = $100/yr; Scenario B (narrowing to 3-20 seat retained/engaged search boutiques at $149/mo as an ATS-agnostic Mandate Room) rescued Lucrative to $1,000/yr but shrank Plausible 10x to 10k while staying pinned at Eager-Comparative = 0.1.
- **Root Cause:** Three structural synthesis errors: (1) Software-washing an interpersonal power asymmetry — contingent hiring managers treat non-retained agencies as interchangeable vendors and reject async homework/formal SLAs while practitioners already calibrate for $0 in 20 minutes via live LinkedIn screen-sharing, and retained partners charging $25k-$100k retainers refuse to alienate C-suite clients with automated '48-hour SLA req-pause' threats; (2) Disconnected sidecar double-entry trap — stripping ATS integration and PII to chase Liquid = 1.0 and DP22 broke the workflow seam, preventing Scenario A from detecting real submittal delays and forcing Scenario B researchers to keep manually copying candidate statuses out of Invenias/FileFinder/Bullhorn while Longlist.io ($47/u/mo), Candidately (~$39-$49/u/mo), Formatix.AI, and Motion.io ($51/mo) bracketed the price/feature floor and ceiling; and (3) Bimodal buyer straddling — counting 0-4 employee solo desks (64-67% of UK/US agencies spending $20-$220/mo total) to hit Plausible = 100k while assuming multi-seat agency $1,000/yr add-on budgets.
- **Machine Fix for Future Runs:** Add three Phase 2 pre-scoring gates: (1) an External-Party Participation Gate that rejects software-enforced SLAs/homework aimed at power-asymmetric vendor-client relationships where a $0 live call outperforms async links; (2) a Sidecar Seam Audit requiring ATS/CRM-agnostic tools to prove how downstream state updates without manual double-entry; and (3) a Bimodal TAM/ACV Check anchoring Lucrative strictly to the median-sized firm representing the bulk of Plausible rather than combining soloists' headcount with multi-seat agencies' software budgets.
