## 4. Candidate Learned Principles (Promote to `principles/LEARNED-PRINCIPLES.md`)

- **Audit Both Floors and Ceilings Before Claiming an Indie Gap:** Before declaring a mid-market or SMB "indie gap," scout BOTH the bottom-up micro-SaaS floor (<$150/mo self-serve tools, app-store utilities, pay-per-run audits) AND the modern vertical-specialist ceiling ($1,000-$2,000/mo month-to-month SaaS), not just legacy enterprise ERPs ($30k-$100k+/yr). Auto-reject any idea sandwiched between sub-$100/mo indie incumbents below and bundled month-to-month specialists above. *(Evidence: Phase 0 lenses benchmarked against enterprise platforms (NetSuite/Fulfil.io, Rewst/ConnectWise, Procore/Textura) and missed the active indie/mid-market layer that killed all 4 ideas in Phase 3: Audit3PL ($29/audit), WarehouseAudit/FulfillAudit ($75-$99/mo), and Implentio ($1k-$2k/mo) sandwiched the 3PL auditor (0.004); CloudOlive/Gradient/Sync 365/BillingBot owned MSP billing (0.01); CIPP/Inforcer/CloudCapsule owned commercial M365 drift (0.20).)*
- **Reject Micro-Niche Escapes Inside Illiquid (Liquid = 0.1) Back-Office Categories:** Never try to rescue a crowded category (Eager-Comparative = 0.1) by narrowing into a 1,000-account micro-niche when buying remains trapped on annual budgets, renewal cycles, or compliance shocks (Liquid = 0.1). With Liquid = 0.1, shrinking Plausible from 10,000 to 1,000 makes clearing the 1.0 indie Problem Score bar mathematically nearly impossible; prioritize Phase 0 lenses with event-driven, always-on buying liquidity (Liquid >= 0.5) over back-office audit/compliance workflows. *(Evidence: All 4 killed ideas scored Liquid = 0.1 in both Scenario A and Scenario B (annual budget cycles, insurance/GL renewals, CUI boundary reviews, billing-shock inertia) and all 4 collapsed to Plausible = 1,000 when narrowing away from incumbents (Extensiv/Deposco CPG, Atera/SuperOps MSPs, GCC High DIB MSPs, NY Scaffold Law QuickBooks GCs), capping best scores at 0.004, 0.01, 0.04, and 0.20.)*
- **Avoid Catastrophic-Liability Transfer Workflows for Unvetted Indie Founders:** Disqualify ideas where a software false-negative exposes the buyer to $1M+ strict legal liability, defense-contract compliance failure, or mid-market financial audit restatement whenever the founder profile is unvetted and lacks institutional trust assets (E&O indemnity, licensed analysts, SOC 2, U.S.-person/CUI credentials). - **Reject Thin Zero-API Utilities Exposed to DIY LLM Scripts and Host-Platform Absorption:** Reject "read-only / zero-API" wedges whose core output is either deterministic CSV/PDF diffing (trivially substituted by DIY Claude/Python scripts) or single-vendor configuration snapshots (steadily absorbed by the host platform's native governance stack). Avoiding write-integration toil is only defensible when paired with proprietary workflow lock-in, multi-party network state, or recurring operational dependency that preserves Enduring >= 1.0. *(Evidence: Invoice-First MSP Billing Auditor (0.01) stripped away live PSA write-back to dodge onboarding friction, leaving a $149/mo CSV diff utility vulnerable to DIY Python/Claude scripts; M365 GCC High Drift Vault (0.20) relied on read-only Microsoft Graph snapshots whose durability was capped at Enduring = 0.5 by expanding native Microsoft UTCM and Purview Compliance Manager capabilities.)*
