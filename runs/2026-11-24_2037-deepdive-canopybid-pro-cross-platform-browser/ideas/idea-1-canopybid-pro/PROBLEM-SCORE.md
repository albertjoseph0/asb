# Problem Score — CanopyBid Pro — Cross-Platform Browser & iPad 2D Horticulture CAD, Regional Plant Palettes & Live Takeoff Proposal Studio

## Scenario A
- **Buyer:** Independent residential landscape/garden designers (solo/micro-firms under NAICS 541320 and non-licensed residential garden designers) and 1-5 person residential design-build contractors (design-build slice of NAICS 561730, excluding pure mow-and-blow maintenance crews and commercial civil/architecture firms) across Windows, macOS, and iPadOS in the U.S., Canada, UK, Australia, and Western Europe who draft 10-40+ scaled 2D residential planting plans, visual plant palettes, and itemized $5k-$75k material-takeoff bids per year.
- **Problem:** Independent residential planting designers and 1-5 person design-build estimators are caught between expensive/clunky desktop-first CAD suites (DynaScape Creator+Color $1,629/yr with no iPad touch/Pencil, Vectorworks Landmark $1,530/yr desktop BIM overkill, Windows-desktop-anchored PRO Landscape+ $900/yr), disconnected manual stacks (Morpholio Trace + PlantMaster + Excel breaking sync on every homeowner plant swap), and consumer/horizontal tools lacking precision Arch-D 2D planting CAD or native regional horticultural databases (Sow Pro+ $99.99/yr, ArcSite $1,188-$1,548/yr, Rayon $252-$456/yr).
- **Trade-offs:** (1) Strictly 2D scaled residential planting/softscape/bed plans and proposals — zero 3D walkthrough rendering, pool/hardscape engineering, grading contours, or irrigation hydraulics; (2) Zero crew dispatch, CRM, invoicing, or payments (exports PDF/CSV/Excel into Jobber/LMN/QuickBooks); (3) Zero unscaled generative AI yard overlays; (4) Strictly residential horticulture (unusable for interior design, MEP, or commercial civil); (5) Standalone single-buyer tool with zero regulated PII/HIPAA/SOC-2/professional-stamp liability.
- **Price:** $69/mo billed annually ($828/yr; or $89/mo monthly) for Solo Pro; $129/mo billed annually ($1,548/yr; or $159/mo monthly) for 3-seat Design-Build Studio (Less for Less)
