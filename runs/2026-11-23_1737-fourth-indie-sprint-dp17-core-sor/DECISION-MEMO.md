## 3. Root-Cause Diagnosis of Killed Ideas

### PatronRail — Web-Only, 0%-Commission Patronage, Private Podcast RSS & Discord Sync Ledger
- **Proximate Cause (Symptom):** Killed at Phase 3 Problem Score = 0.1 (EBI = 0.7, Min = 0.4). Narrowing to high-volume BYO-Stripe Memberful/Supercast podcast networks (1,000-25,000+ paid subs) collapsed Plausible to 1,000 while proprietary private-podcast RSS feed migration kept Liquid = 0.1, capping Scenario B 10x below the 1.0 viability floor despite repricing to $399/mo.
- **Root Cause:** Hidden end-consumer distribution-rail lock-in disguised as a software pricing revolt. Scouting equated creator outrage over Patreon/Memberful/Supercast take-rates with immediate switching demand, overlooking that incumbents lock creators via non-portable consumer rails (stored card tokens + MoR VAT on Patreon; proprietary private RSS URLs inside 1,000-15,000 Apple/Spotify apps on Supercast/Memberful). Dodging card-token lock-in by filtering strictly to BYO-Stripe high-GMV accounts left RSS lock-in intact while collapsing global TAM into a Pareto tail (~1,000-3,000 accounts).
- **Machine Fix for Future Runs:** Insert a mandatory Switching-Rail & Portability Audit in Phase 1/2 for every commission-revolt concept: check whether incumbents control (a) stored end-customer payment tokens / Merchant-of-Record tax compliance or (b) consumer-facing subscriber URLs/feeds/apps. If migrating requires manual action from thousands of end consumers (risking >5-10% silent churn), lock Liquid = 0.1 upfront and reject Pareto-tail micro-niche pivots (<1% of creators) that fail the DP16 arithmetic floor.

## 4. Candidate Learned Principles (Promote to `principles/LEARNED-PRINCIPLES.md`)

- **Audit End-Consumer Switching Rails & Separate Marketplace Demand Fees from Workflow SaaS Fees:** Never treat loud complaints about an incumbent's commission or price hike as evidence of Liquid = 1.0 switching velocity. Before synthesizing any flat-fee or 0%-commission replacement for a core B2B2C system-of-record, audit four end-consumer switching rails: (1) stored customer payment tokens and Merchant-of-Record tax liability, (2) proprietary consumer URLs/apps/feeds (private RSS domains, native studio booking apps), (3) embedded demand-generation surfaces (consumer marketplaces, Google Business Profile/website booking widgets), and (4) annual auto-renew contracts plus staff retraining. Never position a 0%-commission workflow tool as a replacement for a marketplace take-rate that buyers pay for inbound customer acquisition; if switching risks >5% end-consumer churn or lost demand, assign Liquid = 0.1 upfront. *(Evidence: All 4 kills in this run hit Liquid = 0.1 from consumer-side rails rather than software features: PatronRail (Patreon non-portable cards/MoR VAT cliff + Supercast/Memberful proprietary private-podcast RSS URLs forcing 1,000-15,000 listeners to re-subscribe), BanquetBook (Peerspace's 15-20% fee buys consumer lead-gen, not BEO templates; Tripleseat 1-yr auto-renew + Toast POS bundling), MatLedger (Mindbody/Vagaro PCI token lock-in & consumer app), and VanVoice (embedded GBP/website widgets, saved deposit cards, recurring 4-6 week calendars).)*
- **Reject Greenfield 'New-Launch' Escapes from Illiquid Systems-of-Record (The Stock-vs-Flow Trap):** When a core system-of-record stalls at Liquid = 0.1 due to incumbent migration friction, forbid Scenario B pivots to newly launching ('greenfield') businesses unless the annual flow of net-new independent launches (excluding franchises with mandated software) clears the DP16 arithmetic floor (Annual Flow x Lucrative >= $10M at Liquid = 1.0). Narrowing a large stock market to annual new-business incorporations collapses Plausible by 10x-50x while concentrating on high-capex opening-day buyers who refuse unvetted solo software (Eager-Identity = 0.1). To win Liquid = 1.0 inside DP17 categories, target per-project, per-event, or non-rip-and-replace operational workflows adopted fresh on the buyer's next job rather than once-per-decade business openings. *(Evidence: MatLedger Scenario B pivoted to newly launching independent Reformer Pilates studios to dodge Mindbody migration lock-in, collapsing Plausible 10x+ to ~1,000-3,000 non-franchise openings/yr (after stripping Club Pilates/JetSet/STRONG franchises), driving Eager-Identity to 0.1 among founders risking $80k-$250k in buildout capex, and cratering Problem Score to 0.0008.)*
- **Treat Famous Flagship Pricing Revolts as Lagging Indicators & Pre-Screen Sub-$60 Vertical Floors and POS Bundles:** Treat viral Reddit/forum anger at flagship SMB or creator incumbents (Mindbody, Tripleseat, Jobber, MoeGo, Patreon) as a lagging indicator that $0-$60/mo vertical specialists and native POS modules (Toast, Square, Clover) have already saturated the price floor. Before Phase 2 drafts any Less-for-Less or More-for-Less pricing-revolt concept, Phase 1 must run explicit floor-and-bundle discovery queries ('free <vertical> software', '<vertical> software under $50', '<POS> <workflow> add-on') and disqualify pure flat-fee undercutting on sight. Earning Eager-Comparative >= 0.5 in a large category requires an uncopyable operational or structural advantage incumbents cannot match, not a $59-$69/mo price tag. *(Evidence: All 4 ideas suffered floor saturation missed during Phase 1/2 scouting: BanquetBook collided with MyVenueHQ ($0-$49/mo), Perfect Venue ($0/mo), HeyBEO (free), and Toast Catering ($100/mo); MatLedger hit Fit Viz (free), Bobclass (<$35/mo), Bookify Pilates ($50-$100/mo), and Vibefam ($89/mo); VanVoice hit PetRoute ($29/mo), Groomify ($39/van), ARI ($40-$60/mo), DetailerOS ($50/mo), and ServiceM8 ($29-$79/mo); PatronRail Scenario A hit Whop/Subscord/Fourthwall.)*
- **Ban Artificial Multi-Trade Horizontal Bundling & Audit Compliance Workarounds for Core-Workflow Regression:** Never inflate Plausible by stitching multiple distinct physical trades into a single thin horizontal CRM when sub-$60/mo vertical specialists already embed trade-mandatory primitives (OBD/PartsTech/labor guides, vaccine waivers, CARFAX).
