# Problem Score — ShelfPitch: CPG Brand & Broker True-Scale Straw-Man Shelf-Set, UNFI/KeHE Margin-Stack & Buyer-Pitch Studio

ID: `IDEA-3` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-9, SIG-10, SIG-11, SIG-12

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Emerging and mid-market independent CPG brand founders/sales leads (food, beverage, beauty, pet, specialty) and boutique/regional CPG food & specialty brokers pitching regional grocers, natural chains (Whole Foods LEAP, Sprouts Forager, co-ops), and specialty retail category buyers.
- **Problem:** Independent CPG brands and regional brokers pitching regional grocers, natural chains, and specialty retail buyers lack a unified browser tool combining true-to-inch straw-man shelf-set visuals, UNFI/KeHE distributor margin-stack waterfalls (COGS -> FOB -> distributor markup -> MCBs/free-fills/slotting -> retailer margin & profit per linear foot), case-pack days-of-supply checks, and buyer-ready PDF/PPTX sell sheets and pitch decks—forcing them to hack unscaled Canva/PowerPoint mocks alongside manual Excel margin sheets.
- **Trade-offs:** Explicitly excludes 1-10 store c-stores/liquor/independent grocers (anti-market on free DSD planograms/Vori/Petrosoft/PDI); omits full-store 10,000-SKU aisle CAD, mobile compliance auditing, POS/ERP inventory sync, and enterprise Blue Yonder .psa automation; relies solely on buyer-uploaded SKU data/images (no third-party SKU library or live UNFI/KeHE EDI feeds); bakes in opinionated UNFI/KeHE/direct trade-spend waterfall math and templated PDF/PPTX buyer pitch exports.
- **Price:** $79/mo per independent CPG brand ($790/yr annual) or $149/mo per regional broker workspace (up to 15 client brands), month-to-month. (More for More)
