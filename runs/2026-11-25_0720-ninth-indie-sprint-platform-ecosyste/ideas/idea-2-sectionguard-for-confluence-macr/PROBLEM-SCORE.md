# Problem Score — SectionGuard for Confluence (Macro-Free Section Freshness & Rovo Quarantine)

ID: `IDEA-2` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-5, SIG-8

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Confluence Cloud Administrators, Engineering/SRE Operations Leads, and Knowledge Managers at 50-250 seat organizations ($3,100-$42,300/yr Confluence Standard or Premium spend) maintaining multi-team runbooks and SOPs who enabled Atlassian Rovo AI or migrated legacy Data Center wikis into Cloud.
- **Problem:** Confluence Cloud natively tracks lastModified and Page Owner strictly at the whole-page level, so typo fixes or single-paragraph edits reset timestamps across monolithic multi-section runbooks/SOPs and mask 12-24 month stale sections from CQL sweeps and page-level hygiene apps. Because Atlassian Rovo AI ingests every page a user can view (with no native no-ai-index tag and $0.01/credit overages effective Dec 2026), Rovo quotes stale paragraphs inside recently touched pages.
- **Trade-offs:** Uses zero proprietary Confluence content macros (parses native ADF headings via Forge events for zero lock-in); refuses to build whole-page ISO/FDA multi-stage approval workflows, e-signatures, or PDF watermarking (ceding compliance governance to Comala/Breeze/Midori); runs 100% deterministic ADF section hashing and quarantine rules inside Atlassian Forge with zero external LLM API calls ('Runs on Atlassian' zero-egress badge, zero extra Rovo Credit burn).
- **Price:** $2.00/user/month standard Atlassian Marketplace tiers (free <=10 users; $1,200/yr at 50 seats; $2,400/yr at 100 seats; $5,400/yr at 250 seats with Marketplace band tapering; 30-day free trial via Paid via Atlassian). (More for More)
