# Problem Score — PatronRail — Web-Only, 0%-Commission Patronage, Private Podcast RSS & Discord Sync Ledger

ID: `IDEA-1` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario B` · Signals: SIG-6, SIG-10

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Independent podcasters, YouTube video essayists, and paid-Discord hosts with 200+ paying monthly members ($1,000-$25,000+/mo recurring patronage) currently paying Patreon's 10% fee plus Apple's Nov 2026 30% iOS IAP cut, Memberful's $49/mo + 4.9% fee, Fourthwall's 5% membership fee, or Supercast's $0.59/subscriber/mo fee.
- **Problem:** Once a podcaster or Discord host crosses 200-500 paying members, percentage take-rates and Apple's Nov 1, 2026 mandatory iOS IAP migration take $2,000-$25,000+/yr (10% Patreon + 30% Apple iOS or ~43% iOS price hikes, 4.9% Memberful, 5% Fourthwall, or $0.59/sub Supercast), while budget 0%-fee tip jars (Ko-fi Gold at $6-$12/mo) lack revocable private podcast RSS hosting, reliable multi-tier Discord role reconciliation, and Patreon tier migration. Incumbents cannot copy flat $79/mo 0%-fee web-only billing without destroying 80-95% of their take-rate revenue and abandoning their consumer apps.
- **Trade-offs:** Deliberately ships zero native iOS/Android consumer app (avoiding Apple's 30% IAP cut at the cost of mobile app-store discovery/UX); no marketplace, recommendation feed, or social discovery (relies entirely on creators bringing their own audience); BYO Stripe Standard only (eliminates platform payout freezes/MoR underwriting, but shifts global VAT/sales-tax registration/filing and chargeback disputes directly onto the creator); and no native video hosting/transcoding (embeds unlisted YouTube/Vimeo/Bunny streams to keep unit costs flat at $79/mo).
- **Price:** $79/mo flat (up to 5,000 paying members; $149/mo above 5,000; 0% platform commission, BYO Stripe) (More for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** High-volume independent podcast networks, podcast-newsletter hybrids, and multi-show media brands with 1,000–25,000+ active paid subscribers ($5,000–$100,000+/mo GMV) already running on BYO-Stripe Memberful ($49/mo + 4.9%) or Supercast ($0.59/active sub/mo) — especially low-to-mid ARPU ($3–$8/mo) shows paying $7,000–$70,000+/yr in platform fees.
- **Problem:** High-volume independent podcast networks and paid media brands with 1,000–25,000+ active paid subscribers ($5,000–$100,000+/mo GMV) running on BYO-Stripe Memberful ($49/mo + 4.9% = $6,000–$60,000+/yr) or Supercast ($0.59/sub/mo = $7,080–$177,000+/yr — especially punishing on $3–$5/mo tiers at 12%–20% effective take-rate) already own their Stripe customer/card tokens and tax setup, yet bleed tens of thousands per year in per-subscriber or rev-share fees because flat-fee podcast hosts (Transistor Enterprise, Castos, Captivate) lack turnkey zero-card-churn Memberful/Supercast Stripe-subscription takeover, unified 1-click listener RSS re-onboarding portals, and multi-tier Discord role sync.
- **Trade-offs:** Abandons Patreon creators (<1,000 members and non-portable payment cards), small hobbyists, and pure-Discord servers completely; builds exclusively as a zero-card-churn BYO-Stripe takeover + private podcast RSS + Discord role sync off-ramp for existing Memberful/Supercast accounts; zero native consumer app, zero Merchant of Record tax intermediation (buyers already manage tax on their own Stripe account), and zero video hosting/transcoding.
- **Price:** $399/mo flat (up to 15,000 active paid subscribers; $599/mo up to 30,000; 0% platform commission, BYO Stripe with turnkey Memberful/Supercast Stripe-subscription takeover) (More for Less)
