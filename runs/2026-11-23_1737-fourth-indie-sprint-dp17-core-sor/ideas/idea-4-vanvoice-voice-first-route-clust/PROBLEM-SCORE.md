# Problem Score — VanVoice — Voice-First, Route-Clustered Field Ops for 1-5 Person Mobile Driveway Crews

ID: `IDEA-4` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-4, SIG-5

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Owner-operators of 1-5 person mobile driveway service crews — specifically mobile auto detailers, mobile pet groomers, and mobile mechanics running 1-3 service vans/trucks — who hit $100-$239/mo 2-user/2-van cliffs on Jobber, MoeGo, Urable, OrbisX, or QuoteIQ, or hack together Google Calendar + Apple Notes + Zelle.
- **Problem:** The moment a solo mobile detailer, pet groomer, or mechanic hires a first helper or adds a second van, incumbents hit them with a 2x-4x pricing cliff ($100-$239/mo) plus $35/mo A2P texting add-ons and 2.9%-3.4% card fees inside desktop-first dispatcher forms, while generic <$50/mo calendars (Square/Setmore/Acuity) fail to cluster driveway routes or track recurring vehicle/pet condition history.
- **Trade-offs:** Voice-first thumb-operated mobile workflow only (rejecting desktop dispatcher boards, inventory/ERP, and franchise reporting); flat $59/mo for up to 5 crew / 3 vans; personal-device one-tap sms: ETA & review links instead of A2P 10DLC automated texting; payment-rail agnostic logging (Zelle, Venmo, cash, check, BYO Stripe with 0% platform markup).
- **Price:** $59/mo flat per business (up to 5 crew members / 3 vans; 0% payment lock-in supporting Zelle, Venmo, cash, or BYO Stripe) (More for Less)

- **Buyer:** Owner-operators of independent 2-5 van mobile pet grooming fleets (plus 1-van operators actively scaling to van #2) in US/CA/UK/AU currently paying $198-$795/mo on MoeGo's per-van tiers or struggling with cellular dead-zone van outages and zone-day scheduling.
- **Problem:** Independent 2-5 van mobile pet grooming fleets (and 1-van operators adding van #2) face steep per-van pricing cliffs and SMS caps on MoeGo ($99-$159/van = $198-$795/mo for 2-5 vans) plus cellular dead-zone app failures in suburban/exurban vans, while low-cost calendars lack breed/coat/rabies-vaccine records, 4-to-6-week Doodle auto-rebooking, and zone-day zip-clustered routing.
- **Trade-offs:** Built exclusively for mobile pet grooming fleets (dropping auto detailers, mobile mechanics, and brick-and-mortar salons/boarding); flat $99/mo for up to 5 vans with unlimited groomers; offline-first mobile van app with voice-logged breed/coat/matting notes, rabies-vaccine cards, 4-to-6-week auto-rebook, zone-day zip-code booking, and built-in SMS reminders (dropping the broken personal-phone sms: workaround).
- **Price:** $99/mo flat per fleet (up to 5 vans, unlimited groomers, built-in SMS reminders, 0% payment markup via BYO Stripe/Zelle/cash) (More for Less)
