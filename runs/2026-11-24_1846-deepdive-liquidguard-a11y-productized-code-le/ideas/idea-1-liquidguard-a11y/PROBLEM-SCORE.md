# Problem Score — LiquidGuard A11y — Productized Code-Level WCAG 2.2 AA Remediation & Monthly Regression-PR Guard for Shopify & WooCommerce Stores

## Scenario A
- **Buyer:** Owners and ecommerce managers of independent US and EU Shopify (standard OS 2.0 themes: Dawn, Impulse, Prestige) and WooCommerce (standard/block themes: Astra, GeneratePress) stores generating $250k-$5M/yr GMV (approx. 2-19 employees, 10k-150k monthly visitors) seeking source-code WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility remediation without JavaScript overlay widgets.
- **Problem:** Automated $49-$479/mo JavaScript overlay widgets (accessiBe, UserWay) fail screen-reader tests, triggered the 2025 FTC $1M deceptive-advertising order against accessiBe, and fail to block ADA Title III lawsuits (5,100+ filings in 2025, ~70% ecommerce, 22%-40% hitting sites with widgets); automated scanners ($29-$299/mo) report errors without patching Liquid/PHP theme code; and managed code-remediation providers (TestParty $1k-$5k/mo; full-service agencies $15k-$50k+) price above independent $250k-$5M stores.
- **Trade-offs:** (1) Standard Shopify OS 2.0 and WooCommerce standard/block themes only—refuses headless React/Hydrogen builds, custom ERP checkouts, and Fortune 500 procurement; (2) zero runtime JavaScript overlay widgets injected; (3) explicitly disclaims '100% lawsuit immunity' and legal indemnity—delivers source-code WCAG 2.2 AA staging PRs, NVDA/VoiceOver QA recordings, and dated conformance logs only; (4) staging theme/Collaborator code access only—zero customer PII, payment, or order-database custody; (5) month-to-month cancel anytime with zero annual contracts.
- **Price:** $1,450 flat 10-day initial theme remediation sprint + $299/mo month-to-month continuous monitoring, weekly regression PRs, and quarterly manual NVDA/VoiceOver spot-check videos ($5,038 Year-1 / $3,588/yr recurring). (Less for Less)
