## Root-Cause Persistence
- Proximate symptoms (the 2025 FTC $1M order against accessiBe, 5,100+ annual ADA filings hitting ~70%-79% ecommerce, and TestParty's $1k-$5k/mo enterprise pricing) create the illusion of an underserved $250k-$5M SMB gap. Independent verification reveals three non-indie-favorable structural root causes for why 95%+ of SMB stores remain non-compliant despite $29-$200/mo code-fixing apps (AccessComply, Patrol, Perceivable) already existing: (1) Rational SMB apathy & low perceived base-rate risk (DP4)—outside the ~3%-5%/yr cohort hit by demand letters, $250k-$5M owners prioritize revenue-generating spend over compliance, and EU microenterprises (<10 employees AND <=EUR 2M turnover) are legally exempt from EAA e-commerce service obligations; (2) Architectural platform boundary—Liquid/PHP theme PRs literally cannot remediate client-side JS, Shadow DOM, and cross-origin iframes injected by the 15-30 third-party revenue apps (Klaviyo, Recharge, Yotpo) that cause most real-world screen-reader failures and regressions; and (3) Credence-good / legal-defense purchasing dynamics (DP10)—when merchants finally pay $3k-$10k+, they buy institutional risk transfer, IAAP WAS/CPACC authority, disabled-user testing panels, and litigation-defense backing required by settlement decrees, not unindemnified theme PRs from an unvetted solo founder. *(Indie-favorable: `False`)*

## 1st / 2nd / 3rd-Order Consequences

- Immediate technical scope failures on the first 5-10 customer stores: real $250k-$5M Shopify/WooCommerce stores run 15-30 third-party apps (Klaviyo popups/focus traps, Recharge subscription iframes, Yotpo review/star widgets, chat/cookie banners) that inject client-side JS, Shadow DOM, and cross-origin iframes outside Liquid/PHP theme files; theme-only PRs cannot remediate those widgets, forcing either embarrassing exclusions on the conformance log or unbillable custom JS mutation-observer hacks that break on vendor updates.
- Severe outbound/self-serve acquisition friction: outside the ~3%-5%/yr cohort actively holding an ADA demand letter or facing an agency redesign, $250k-$5M merchants treat WCAG as priority #7 (95.9% of top homepages still fail; <2.3% of Shopify Plus even installs accessiBe), EU stores under 10 staff and <=EUR 2M turnover are statutorily exempt from EAA services rules, and Q4 BFCM ecommerce code freezes (mid-Oct through Dec, DP24) stall theme PR merges for ~20%-25% of the calendar year.
