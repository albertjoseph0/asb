# Problem Score — ProofSlate — ATS-Agnostic Verified Candidate Submittal Card & Fee-Ownership Ledger

ID: `IDEA-5` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-15

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Managing partners and desk-owning recruiters at 1-10 recruiter boutique technical, finance, and executive search agencies placing salaried or contract roles on $10k-$40k contingency or retained fees who currently email static PDF resumes or spreadsheets to hiring managers.
- **Problem:** AI-generated keyword-stuffed resumes, real-time AI interview copilots, and proxy/deepfake remote candidates erode hiring manager trust in static PDF submittals, causing slow feedback cycles and duplicate-submittal fee disputes, while incumbent agency ATS portals (Loxo, Crelate, Recruiterflow, Recruit CRM) and presentation tools (Candidately, Hinterview, Odro) often rely on per-seat pricing, annual contracts, or ATS lock-in.
- **Trade-offs:** Standalone submittal layer (refuses to replace ATS/CRM/email sequencer—no two-way database migration); no biometric facial recognition, government-ID KYC storage, or legal deepfake warranty (uses a 60-second unedited live recruiter-candidate vetting clip + spoken-vs-resume discrepancy flags); zero hiring-manager login/password gate (expiring magic links with cryptographic view/approval timestamps and Cal.com/Calendly booking).
- **Price:** $99/mo flat per agency (up to 5 recruiters, month-to-month, unlimited submittals) and $179/mo (up to 15 recruiters, custom submittal domain) — $1,188-$2,148/yr (More for Less)

## Scenario B — Narrowed Niche
- **Buyer:** Owners and recruiting directors at 5-25 recruiter direct-client (non-VMS) remote IT contract staffing and technical staff-augmentation agencies placing remote W2/C2C software, cloud, and AI engineers at startups and mid-market companies.
- **Problem:** Direct-client remote IT contract staffing agencies face revenue-destroying proxy interviewees, real-time AI coding copilots, virtual-camera deepfakes, and day-one bait-and-switch engineer swaps that trigger immediate client contract cancellations, while enterprise proctoring suites (Glider AI) require heavy annual procurement and background-check vendors (ClearStar) only verify at onboarding rather than embedding live vetting proof into client submittals.
- **Trade-offs:** Standalone ATS-agnostic vetting-and-submittal packet explicitly excluding Fortune 500 VMS/MSP portals (Fieldglass/Beeline) and ATS replacement; replaces passive 60-second bio clips with a 3-minute live recruiter-guided technical walkthrough + start-day continuity snapshot with virtual-camera/IP/resume-claim telemetry, while still refusing BIPA biometric facial-recognition storage and strict-liability legal fraud warranties (DP10).
