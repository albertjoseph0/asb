# Problem Score — Live Ad-Spend vs. Usage-Rights Watchdog & 14-Day Spark/Partnership Renewal Desk

ID: `IDEA-1` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-6, SIG-7, SIG-8, SIG-9, SIG-10

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** Media-buying directors and account leads at boutique paid-social/performance-creative agencies managing 5-40 client ad accounts, plus in-house paid-social leads at $1M-$25M Shopify/TikTok-Shop DTC brands running at least 15 active Meta Partnership Ads or TikTok Spark Ads per month.
- **Problem:** Paid-social teams scaling past 15 licensed UGC assets face two operational failures: (1) TikTok Spark Ads Error 4025 abruptly halts live ads when 7/30/60/365-day auth codes expire (requiring fresh codes and ad rebuilds), and Meta Partnership Ads permission drops (#4289001 / revoked links) silently stop delivery; (2) Meta never pauses active ads when 30/60/90-day contractual usage rights expire, creating ghost-spend overrun fee risk ($5k-$25k+ via Meta Ad Library audits) while teams miss the 14-day pre-expiry renewal window on high-ROAS winners.
- **Trade-offs:** Refuses to bundle a creator discovery marketplace, influencer payout escrow, or gifting CRM (zero GMV take rate); provides zero legal counsel, DMCA defense, or contractual indemnification (strictly operational alerting against user-entered contract dates); skips one-time onboarding links (Leadsie) and creative swipe/analytics suites (Foreplay/Motion); and excludes YouTube, Snapchat, Pinterest, SOC 2 security questionnaires, and enterprise procurement to remain self-serve OAuth + credit card.
- **Price:** $179/mo (Brand: up to 5 ad accounts, 75 active licensed assets) or $349/mo (Agency: up to 25 client ad accounts, 300 active assets); month-to-month credit card, zero % GMV take rates. (More for More)
