# Problem Score — ReplayPipe — Non-Rip-and-Replace Post-Webinar Interactive Replay Rooms & Chapter-Intent CRM Sync

ID: `IDEA-3` · Ambition: `indie` · Active Scenario: `Scenario A` · Signals: SIG-10

## Target Market (Scenario A)
- **Buyer:** 1-15 person B2B marketing, demand-generation, and RevOps teams at B2B SaaS, IT services, and B2B consulting firms running 1+ monthly webinars on Zoom Webinars, Microsoft Teams, or Riverside with HubSpot or Salesforce as their CRM (excluding solo hobbyist course creators).
- **Problem:** Live B2B webinar attendance sits around 37-44% while 40-50% of consumption occurs on-demand, yet Zoom's native HubSpot integration fails to sync on-demand replay registrations or watch time. Lean B2B teams want interactive, chaptered on-demand webinar replays with CRM attribution without migrating live hosting or paying Wistia Business + Signals ($329/mo annual), Vimeo Studio ($400/mo), Vidyard Teams ($99/user/mo), or Cvent-acquired Goldcast/ON24 ($20k-$100k+/yr). However, live floor research reveals that HubSpot Native Video ($0 incremental on Marketing Hub Pro), SproutVideo ($12-$72/mo, HubSpot included on all tiers), Spotlightr ($13-$49/mo), and Contrast ($79+/mo, supports pre-recorded uploads) already solve 80%+ of this replay-attribution job at or below ReplayPipe's $99/mo price.
- **Trade-offs:** Refuses to build live streaming/webinar hosting (keeps Zoom/Teams/Riverside for zero broadcast-day migration risk), refuses general brand video CMS or social clip editing, offers flat $99/mo workspace pricing (unlimited viewers/seats, up to 25 active rooms) instead of per-seat or $329+/mo Signals CRM paywalls, and syncs only non-regulated B2B marketing engagement events via scoped OAuth webhooks.
- **Price:** $99/month flat per workspace (unlimited replay viewers, unlimited seats, native HubSpot/Salesforce/Slack chapter-intent sync, up to 25 active replay rooms) or $990/year (Less for Less)
